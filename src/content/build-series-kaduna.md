# From Ideas to Innovation: How the BlockCapitol Build Series Brought Emerging Builders Together in Kaduna

**Great ideas are everywhere. What often makes the difference is having the right environment, the right people, and the opportunity to turn those ideas into something meaningful.**

That was the thinking behind the **BlockCapitol Build Series**, a three-day innovation programme organised by BlockCapitol in collaboration with **Africa’s Talking Developers Community Kaduna**. Held in Kaduna, Nigeria, the event brought together aspiring developers, entrepreneurs, designers, students, and technology enthusiasts with a shared interest in exploring how technology can solve practical problems in their communities.

The programme was built around a simple idea: learning about technology is important, but knowing how to apply it to real-world challenges is what creates meaningful impact. Over three days, participants explored emerging technologies, exchanged ideas, worked collaboratively, and developed solutions focused on improving everyday experiences for individuals and businesses.

With 40 registrations and 30 participants attending at least one session, the event recorded a 75% registration-to-attendance conversion rate. Attendance reached 20 participants on the opening day, 12 on the second day, and 30 on the final day. Beyond the numbers, the programme created an opportunity for individuals with different backgrounds and levels of experience to come together, learn from one another, and engage in practical innovation.

## Learning Beyond the Classroom

Throughout the programme, participants were introduced to concepts and tools across artificial intelligence, blockchain and Web3, digital product development, and design thinking. The sessions were designed to connect technical knowledge with practical applications, encouraging participants to look beyond emerging technology trends and consider how these tools could address genuine needs.

Contributions from technology professionals, including Shamsudeen Kamardeen, Amrah Uthman Sali, Bello Abdullahi, Abdullahi Yunus, and Sanusi Abdulkadir, helped participants explore different aspects of technology development and problem-solving.

An important part of the learning experience was understanding that building useful technology begins long before writing code. It requires identifying a problem, understanding the people affected by it, examining existing alternatives, and deciding which tools are appropriate for developing a solution.

These discussions became particularly relevant as participants moved from learning sessions into collaborative solution development, where the challenge was no longer simply to understand technology but to demonstrate how it could be applied.

## Six Teams, Different Ideas, One Shared Goal

One of the most exciting moments of the Build Series came during the final presentations, when six teams stepped forward to showcase the solutions they had developed.

The teams focused on two areas with meaningful opportunities for digital innovation: **Property Technology (PropTech) and Local Business Digitisation**.

Both areas reflect challenges that people and businesses encounter regularly, from managing residential properties and finding suitable accommodation to helping small businesses attract and retain customers.

For the participating teams, the presentations represented more than an opportunity to showcase an idea. They offered a chance to articulate a problem, communicate a proposed solution, and share the results of their collaborative efforts with an audience.

The presentations also demonstrated something important about innovation: meaningful ideas do not have to begin with large teams, extensive resources, or sophisticated infrastructure. Sometimes, progress begins when a few people come together, recognise a problem, and commit to finding a better way of addressing it.

## Meet the Winning Teams: Three Solutions Addressing Everyday Challenges

At the conclusion of the programme, three teams stood out for their creativity, the relevance of their solutions, and their potential to address real-world challenges. **Team PropFix, Team Slotwise, and Team Streak** were recognised for their efforts, receiving cash awards totalling **₦180,000**.

While each team approached a different challenge, their solutions shared a common objective: making everyday processes simpler, more efficient, and more accessible through technology.

### Team PropFix: Making Property Maintenance More Reliable

For many landlords and tenants, property maintenance can become a source of frustration. A leaking pipe, faulty electrical installation, or damaged fixture may appear to be a straightforward repair, but finding reliable artisans and coordinating the work can quickly become complicated. Poorly executed repairs can cause further property damage, while unclear communication can lead to disputes between landlords and tenants.

Team PropFix developed a solution designed to address these challenges by connecting landlords with verified artisans who can handle maintenance and repair requests raised by tenants.

The proposed platform aims to create a more structured approach to property maintenance, helping landlords oversee repair requests while improving communication with tenants. By facilitating access to verified service providers, PropFix seeks to minimise the risks associated with unskilled or unauthorised artisans, protect property conditions, and reduce avoidable disagreements.

The team's idea reflects how technology can improve an often-overlooked aspect of property management, making maintenance more accountable and less stressful for everyone involved.

### Team Slotwise: Rethinking How People Find and Manage Rental Properties

Finding suitable accommodation can be a time-consuming experience, even when vacant properties are available. Property owners may struggle to reach prospective tenants, while people searching for accommodation often face difficulties accessing reliable listings and navigating rental processes.

Team Slotwise approached this challenge by developing a solution intended to bridge the gap between property owners with vacant apartments and prospective tenants seeking accommodation.

Beyond improving the visibility and accessibility of available properties, Slotwise aims to digitise important aspects of property management. Its proposed features include property supervision, tenant reviews and ratings, and digital payment processes.

By bringing these functions together, the solution seeks to simplify the rental experience while improving transparency and efficiency for property owners and tenants.

Slotwise's presentation highlighted opportunities for digital tools to modernise property transactions and create more organised approaches to managing residential accommodation.

### Team Streak: Helping Small Businesses Turn Customer Loyalty into Growth

Attracting customers is one challenge for a small business. Getting them to return consistently is another.

For many local businesses, repeat customers are an important source of sustainable growth, yet customer engagement and loyalty programmes can be difficult to organise without suitable digital tools.

Team Streak developed a customer engagement and loyalty solution designed to help small businesses strengthen customer relationships and encourage continued patronage.

The platform incorporates a customer activity tracking and reward system that recognises customers based on their consistency and engagement with a business. Through this approach, businesses can encourage repeat purchases and build stronger relationships with their customers.

Streak also seeks to provide businesses with greater visibility by creating opportunities to showcase their products and services to prospective customers.

The team's solution demonstrates how relatively simple digital approaches to customer engagement can help local businesses improve retention, increase visibility, and create stronger connections with the people they serve.
